P03 · SAN PRO system

A connected path from resource to brand and trust.

SAN PRO is designed as a modular food system: each stage must create useful evidence and capability for the next.

System philosophy

A protein number alone does not build a food business.

The value chain only works if the starting material is qualified, the process is credible, the ingredient functions in real foods, products satisfy sensory and production requirements, and public claims remain inside what evidence can support.

1. Resourcequalify the input
2. Knowledgedefine gaps
3. Technologyvalidate process
4. Ingredientprove function
5. Foodtaste + process
6. Brandtranslate value
7. Trustprove before claim
M0

Infrastructure

The physical and technical platform: a modular production campus and capabilities designed to support accountable projects.

M1

Ingredient platform

Qualified defatted sunflower meal is the starting basis for developing a food ingredient; final specification and yield remain evidence-gated.

M2

Bakery

Dark and wholegrain breads and bruschetta formats are developed as distinct product economics, not as automatic extensions of M1.

M3

Sweet

Wafers, biscuits and related formats form an independent development platform where taste, texture and manufacturability remain primary.

M4

Savoury

Corn tortilla/nacho-style protein chips lead the savoury development path; other crunchy formats remain optional until validated.

Independence and shared capability

Five investment logics, one disciplined development philosophy.

M0–M4 are treated as separate projects/companies with separate investors and responsibilities. Shared or managed capabilities are used only where they are lawful, contractually clean and measurably useful; they do not imply common ownership or hidden consolidation.

Current M1 modelling basis: one working shift · 45 t qualified defatted sunflower meal per working day. No second-shift multiplier is included in the base case.
