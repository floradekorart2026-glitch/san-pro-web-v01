P05 · Quality & trust

Trust is designed before the first product.

Quality starts with the input and process design, continues through control and traceability, and reaches communication: a claim is only as strong as the evidence behind it.

Quality by Design

Build quality into the system

Raw-material specifications, hygienic design, process controls and acceptance criteria should be defined before routine production, not added after problems appear.

Supplier qualification

Know the starting material

The future M1 ingredient can only be as credible as the qualification, variability control and traceability of its starting material.

HACCP · GHP · GMP

Food-safety fundamentals

Hazard analysis, hygiene and good manufacturing practice are baseline operating disciplines, not marketing decorations.

Laboratory & QC

Measure what matters

Incoming material, in-process controls, finished-product testing and external accredited laboratories are used where appropriate to support evidence.

Traceability & CAPA

Detect, contain, learn

Traceability, recall readiness and corrective/preventive action turn quality events into controlled learning rather than isolated incidents.

Certification roadmap

Roadmap is not certification

ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, BRCGS, IFS and related schemes may be evaluated where relevant. SAN PRO does not claim certification before it is actually achieved.

Claims control

Final-product evidence first

Nutrition and other regulated claims must be assessed on the actual final SKU, applicable law and supporting analysis; ingredient assumptions do not automatically transfer to a product.

Regulatory gates

Product and process facts before legal conclusions

Ingredient identity, process history, Novel Food assessment, customs classification and market-specific requirements remain product-specific until formally closed.
