P04 · Products

Food people should genuinely want to eat.

Nutritional ambition matters, but it cannot compensate for poor taste, texture or everyday usefulness. SAN PRO develops applications where the ingredient must earn its place in the product.

M1 · Ingredient

A functional ingredient platform

The future ingredient must be judged by composition, safety, processability, sensory effects and application performance—not by protein percentage alone.

M2 · Bakery

Dark and wholegrain breads

Familiar bakery formats offer a practical route for testing ingredient functionality, texture, taste and consumer relevance.

M2 · Bruschetta

Baked and dried formats

Bruschetta-style products extend the bakery platform into shelf-stable, crunchy applications with distinct process and packaging requirements.

M3 · Sweet

Wafers and biscuits

Sweet formats test whether nutritional ambition can coexist with the texture and enjoyment consumers expect from the category.

M4 · Savoury

Corn tortilla/nacho-style protein chips

The primary savoury path focuses on a familiar crunchy format while recipe, extrusion/baking/frying choices, seasoning and shelf-life remain development variables.

R&D

The product is not finished until it survives reality

Recipe, taste, texture, line compatibility, microbiology, shelf-life, packaging and economics must converge before a concept becomes a launchable SKU.

MIN–MAX philosophy

Minimize avoidable compromise. Maximize useful food value.

MIN–MAX is a development philosophy, not a health claim. It asks every product team to balance taste, texture, nutrition, processing, price, packaging and real-life usefulness rather than optimize one number at the expense of the whole product.
