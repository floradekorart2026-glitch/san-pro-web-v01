Why SAN PRO exists

Create more useful value from resources that already exist.

The starting question is practical: can a qualified food-industry resource become a platform for higher-value ingredients, better everyday foods and capabilities that remain in the system? SAN PRO is designed to test that question through evidence, product reality and economics rather than assumption.

Root and responsibility

Rooted in Slavonia. Designed for demanding markets.

Slavonia is the project's real agricultural and industrial context, not a decorative story. SAN PRO aims to keep a clear regional identity while treating regulation, food safety, technical validation and customer requirements as design inputs from the beginning.

Who we are →
M1 · Ingredient

Sunflower protein ingredient platform

Development starts from qualified defatted sunflower meal and asks whether process, composition and functionality can support a credible food ingredient.

M2 · Bakery

Dark and wholegrain food formats

Bread and bruschetta directions translate ingredient functionality into familiar everyday foods while keeping taste and processability central.

M3 · Sweet

Sweet programme

Wafers, biscuits and related formats are developed as a platform where sensory quality, manufacturability and nutritional ambition must work together.

M4 · Savoury

Crunchy savoury programme

Corn tortilla/nacho-style protein chips are the primary development path, with other savoury options remaining gated until evidence supports them.

Quality

Trust is designed

Supplier qualification, hygienic design, laboratory control, traceability, CAPA and independent verification are designed into the system.

Learning

A system that gets smarter

Human responsibility, structured research, R&D, digital tools and AI are used to shorten learning cycles without lowering the evidence threshold.

Development basis: where M1 operating context is relevant, the current project model uses one working shift and 45 t of qualified defatted sunflower meal per working day. This is a modelling basis, not a claim of current production.
